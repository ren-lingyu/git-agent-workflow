usage: git gaw undeploy

Remove local GAW deployment state without changing ordinary branches, history,
.gaw/config, or existing worktrees and their contents. The command removes
refs/gaw/HEAD when safely inspectable, the GAW named hook's repository-local
configuration, and discoverable exact v0.2 symbolic registrations.

Unsafe or unreadable protocol refs are preserved and reported. Such residuals
make the command return nonzero even when other cleanup succeeded. After
explicitly resolving residuals, undeploy may be run again.

Cleanup affects the entire repository's GAW deployment, preserving formal and
Auxiliary branches/history/worktrees. The report captures branch/selector/hook
and worktree inventory before cleanup; unavailable inventory is marked unknown
without blocking cleanup. Successful removal of existing hook configuration
warns that GAW reference-transaction protection has been disabled. Failed hook
cleanup never claims protection has been disabled; the warning is nonblocking.
Commit's existing behavior after undeploy remains unchanged.
